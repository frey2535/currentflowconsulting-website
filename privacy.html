Privacy Policy

Certain CurrentFlow applications may allow users to create projects, perform calculations, maintain records, upload files, manage materials, enter business information, or otherwise create and store application content.

The specific information processed depends on the application and the features used.

We may use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain user accounts.
